Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Once you download the app and sync your study decks, you can review flashcards completely offline. Videos require an active connection.

Are my stats synced across devices?

Yes! If you do 50 flashcards on your phone on the train, your desktop app will perfectly reflect that progress the moment you log in.

Is there a Web version?

Yes — a web client is available for testing. For the strongest content protection we still recommend the native apps for daily study.
